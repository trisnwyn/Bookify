Trial balance and the ledger
An opening trial balance lists every ledger account and its balance at a chosen date. It is not a financial
statement. It is a checking device, and a modest one: equal debit and credit columns show that the clerk
has avoided the simplest class of posting error. Equality does not prove that each entry reached the correct
account, that no entry was posted twice on the same side, or that an omission was not balanced by a second
mistake somewhere else.
