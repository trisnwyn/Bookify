Comparing counts
The record can be summarised as counts. The counts below are synthetic: they exist to show a
comparison, not to report an observation.

 Category                                     First pass          Second pass         Change
 Detail recorded                              12                  15                  +3
 Setting recorded                             7                   7                   0
 Warrant recorded                             4                   6                   +2

The totals are small and invented. They show a relationship the prose can state: a longer list of details
does not by itself add warrants. The second pass added details and warrants, but the setting count did
not change.
Read the table as a question about coverage, not as evidence that the sample is better understood. A
count records how many entries were made, not how strong they are.
