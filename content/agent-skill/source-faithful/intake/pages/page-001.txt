Notes on Reading a Sample
A synthetic source for the Bookify agent-skill fixture

A sample arrives
These notes describe one small synthetic case. They are written for a reading exercise, not as a report
of an observation.
A sample is a bounded piece of material selected for inspection. In this case, a sample arrives with two
labels. One label gives its place in a sequence; the other gives a short name. The names disagree. Read
alone, either label looks like an error. Read in order, one label was written before the sample moved
and the other after. The difference is a change of place, not a contradiction.

  Case note. The origin of the sample is not recorded. It remains unknown. These notes do not supply
  it.

The notes also turn to the record, to three levels of reading, and to the point where the evidence stops.
