The record
A record keeps four fields together: label, time, place, and status. The status says how firm the entry is.

 Field                 Question                              Example entry (synthetic)
 label                 What is it called?                    Two labels disagree
 time                  When was it recorded?                 Before the move; after the move
 place                 Where was it recorded?                Shelf A; shelf B
 status                How firm is the entry?                Observed; unresolved origin

Every entry in the table is invented for this exercise. The table is a checklist, not a measurement.
If a field cannot be filled, write unresolved. An empty field is not a zero and not a guess. The origin in
the case stays unresolved because no record supplies it.
